## Ghost

Use `variant="ghost"` for quiet actions, such as a back arrow beside a page title or an edit icon beside a value. It's transparent and borderless at rest, and shows a light fill on hover. Ghost supports `primary`, `secondary`, and `support` colors.

<Canvas of={IconButtonStories.Ghost} />

<Controls of={IconButtonStories.Ghost} include={["color"]} />

### Ghost sizes

Ghost comes in `md`, `sm`, and `xs`. Use `xs` for an action inline with text: at 20px it's about the height of a line of body text. `xs` is only supported with the ghost variant.
