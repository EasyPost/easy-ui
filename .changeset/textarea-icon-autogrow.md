`<Textarea />` accepts `iconAtStart`, which pins an icon to its first line, and `autoGrow`, which grows the field with its content from `rows`. Add `maxRows` to cap the growth at that many lines, after which the field scrolls. Manual resizing is off while a textarea auto-grows. `iconAtEnd` still isn't supported on a textarea.
