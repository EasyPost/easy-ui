Adds `statusLabels` to `<SplitToggleCard />`, which states the current state under the switch, such as "Enrolled" or "Not enrolled", in a panel sized to the longer label. Also adds `cardVariant="solid"` for use inside another card, which drops the border and uses `neutral.050` and `neutral.025` backgrounds.
