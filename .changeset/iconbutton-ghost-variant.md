feat: `IconButton` adds a `ghost` variant and an `xs` size. Ghost is transparent and borderless with a light fill on hover and a solid fill while its menu or popover is open, in `primary`, `secondary`, and `support`. `xs` is a 24px ghost icon button for actions inline with text or in a table row. `KebabButton` and `DataGrid`'s icon row actions now render a ghost `xs` `IconButton`, so they gain its hover fill and keyboard focus ring.
