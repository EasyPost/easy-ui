feat: `Checkbox` accepts `isRequired`. The input gets `required` and `aria-required`, so assistive technology announces it as required, and the label shows a trailing `*` that is hidden from assistive technology. Like the fields, `Checkbox` also takes `necessityIndicator`: `"icon"` (the default `*`) or `"label"`, which shows "(required)" or "(optional)".
